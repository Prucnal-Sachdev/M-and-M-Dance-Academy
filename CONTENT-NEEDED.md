# Content needed from M & M Dance Academy

A homepage preview built from what is publicly on mmdanceacademy.com as of 2026-09-05.
Nothing on the page is invented. Below is everything unconfirmed, left out, or worth a
decision before this goes near their domain.

## Things on their current site they will want to know about

- **Sixteen unedited template placeholders.** Their homepage carousel displays
  **"Slide title / Write your caption here / Button"** sixteen times over, as visible page
  text. It is the first thing below the header. None of it is on this preview.
- **No copyright year anywhere.** The only "copyright" on the site is a block of legal
  boilerplate about republication, with no year. Added here, computed at build time.
- **Eight deprecated `<font>` tags**, which have been obsolete since HTML4.
- Their platform is **Thryv**, so there is a paid subscription behind the current site.

Worth saying plainly: **their title tag is fine** — `Dance School | Monroeville, PA |
M & M Dance Academy` is a good one. Do not tell them their SEO is broken; it is not.

## The big gap: nothing about a class is on the website

This is the thing a parent actually needs and cannot get:

- [ ] **Class times.** No schedule anywhere. A parent cannot find out when the Tuesday
      ballet class for five-year-olds runs without emailing.
- [ ] **Prices.** Not published — not tuition, not costume fees, not recital fees.
- [ ] **How to register.** Fall Registration 2026 and the 2026-27 supply list are
      referenced, but registration is not an online form. It appears to run by email
      and on paper.

The preview says "email or call and we will tell you which class to try", which is honest
about how it works today. If they want the real thing, a published schedule and an online
registration form are the two highest-value additions, in that order.

## Unverified, so not used anywhere on the page

- **Who owns the studio.** The outreach tracker lists Lori Mangene as co-owner, but no
  owner, director or staff member is named anywhere on their public site. The only place
  a name appears at all is the `mailto:Lori.mmdance@hotmail.com` link behind their
  "Email Us Today!" button. Nobody is named on the preview.
- **Which email is preferred.** Two are published: `lmdance5678@gmail.com` (shown as text
  in the header, and given for team and company placement) and
  `Lori.mmdance@hotmail.com` (behind the button). The preview uses the gmail one. Ask.
- **Sunday hours.** Their Contact & Location page lists Mon–Thu and Sat, says Friday is
  closed, and then leaves Sunday blank. The preview shows Sunday as closed. Confirm.

## Photography

Five images are reused from their own site: dancers at the barre for the hero, the
preschool group, the company team, a stage shot, and a costumed group. They are the
studio's own published photographs of their own students, used here only to show them
their own site rebuilt.

If this goes further, get explicit permission for which photographs of children may be
used, and ask whether any families have opted out. That is their call to make, not ours.
